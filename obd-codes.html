Diagnostic Codes

P0487
EMISSION

EGR Throttle Control Circuit "A"

Engine control system has detected a problem related to the EGR throttle control circuit.

Possible Causes: Wiring fault, connector problem, actuator fault or control circuit issue.
Check: Inspect wiring, connectors, actuator operation and related circuits.
P0036
ELECTRICAL

HO2S Heater Control Circuit

The oxygen sensor heater control circuit has detected a fault.

Possible Causes: Open/short wiring, damaged connector, fuse or sensor heater problem.
Check: Check power supply, ground, wiring and heater resistance.
P0100
ENGINE

Mass Air Flow Sensor Circuit

A fault has been detected in the MAF sensor circuit.

Possible Causes: MAF sensor, wiring, connector, intake leak or power supply issue.
Check: Inspect air filter, intake pipe, connector and MAF readings.
P0115
ENGINE

Engine Coolant Temperature Sensor

The engine coolant temperature sensor circuit has a fault.

Possible Causes: ECT sensor, wiring, connector or reference voltage problem.
Check: Inspect sensor wiring and compare live temperature data.
